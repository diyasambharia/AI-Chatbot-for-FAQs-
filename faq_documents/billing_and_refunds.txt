# TechCorp Solutions - Billing, Invoices, and Refund Policy

## Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover), PayPal, and direct wire / ACH transfers for annual enterprise contracts over $2,000.

## Q: Where can I find and download my invoices?
A: Account owners and billing administrators can view and download PDF invoices anytime by navigating to Dashboard > Billing > Invoice History.

## Q: What is your refund policy?
A: We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee for all newly purchased monthly or annual subscription plans. If you are not completely satisfied within the first 30 days of your purchase, contact billing@techcorp.io and we will issue a 100% full refund with no cancellation penalties.

## Q: How do I cancel my subscription?
A: You can cancel your subscription at any time without talking to a representative:
1. Go to Account Settings > Billing & Subscriptions.
2. Click "Manage Plan" and select "Cancel Subscription".
3. Confirm your cancellation. Your account remains active with full access until the end of your prepaid billing period.

## Q: What happens to my data after I cancel?
A: After your subscription ends, your account transitions to a read-only grace period for 30 days so you can export your data and documents. After 30 days, account data is permanently purged in compliance with our data retention schedule.
